🧩 OOP — interview question taxonomy

This track is mid-rebuild into an interview question bank for experienced professionals — roughly 3+ years through senior/lead/architect/principal. What's below is Phase 1 of that rebuild — every concept an interviewer could ask about, grouped by topic — before any tier (Basic/Intermediate/Advanced) or priority (🔥/⭐/🧠) gets assigned, and before any page gets built. Nothing here is live yet; this page exists so it's easy to review as a team before Phase 2 (dedupe/gaps) starts. Full detail — the exact question variants per concept — lives in docs/superpowers/specs/oops/question-taxonomy.md.

Deliberately scenario-driven rather than "four pillars 101" — see the taxonomy doc for the full reasoning.

1. The Four Pillars (quick-recall level) 3 concepts ▾
  • Encapsulation vs. Abstraction
  • Inheritance vs. Composition
  • Polymorphism — compile-time vs. runtime, overloading vs. overriding
2. Interface vs. Abstract Class 1 concept ▾
  • Interface vs. Abstract Class — when to reach for each, can an abstract class implement an interface
3. SOLID 6 concepts ▾
  • Single Responsibility Principle
  • Open/Closed Principle
  • Liskov Substitution Principle
  • Interface Segregation Principle
  • Dependency Inversion Principle
  • SOLID as a Whole — applying all five to one real service
4. Composition vs. Inheritance (in depth) 1 concept ▾
  • Why Prefer Composition Over Inheritance — fragile base class, real design-problem examples
5. Design Patterns in Practice 2 concepts ▾
  • Strategy Pattern vs. Conditional Logic — the payment-processor scenario
  • Factory vs. Dependency Injection
6. Coupling, Cohesion & Maintainability 2 concepts ▾
  • Coupling vs. Cohesion — and what makes code maintainable
  • Refactoring a Tightly Coupled Service
